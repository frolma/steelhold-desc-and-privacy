Standard Issue

Three Campaigns and Endless

  • Steel Crusade: from the first skirmish to the final siege
  • Iron Labyrinth: night missions under fog of war
  • INFERNO: missions of lava, web and bone
  • Endless Onslaught: waves that keep coming until you stop

Your Tank, Your Rank

A hangar dresses your tank: hulls, paint, glow, tracer rounds, muzzle flash, tread dust and emblems, and nothing in it is sold on its own. Your commander level climbs with every mission you clear for the first time and pays out rewards all the way: new abilities, better hardware and promotions up to Marshal. The free missions pay out the first rewards, the Full Version opens the road to Marshal.

Giant Boss Fights

The first run, the boss kills you. The second, you notice the pause before its salvo. The third, it is yours. Every win hangs a plate on your trophy wall in bronze, silver or gold, and a beaten boss can be fought again as an exhibition.

When The Line Is Breaking

Airstrikes, escort drones, auto-turrets, and a base repair that rebuilds your wall with one tap while the shells are still falling. Now and then a supply plane crosses the field and parachutes a crate down for you to reach.

Endless Takes Orders

Orders earned in the campaigns make an Endless run harder and add to its score: heavier hulls, faster hunters, no air cover, shorter breathers. Arm one, arm them all, and see how deep you get.

A Battlefield That Lives

Rowboats tied up at the piers, wire sagging between poles, sandbags and tank traps, craters with wildflowers growing back. Warm lamplight along the roads, and on every map a lamp or two that never light. Those are not a bug. Leave them be.

For The Record

Game Center leaderboards for your furthest mission, your best run and your highest Endless wave on Easy, Medium and Hard. Medals to earn: the first is called First Blood, and one asks for 10,000 kills. Every battle ends with a war dossier, and any victory can go to a friend as a card.

Play It Your Way

Touch controls, a game controller or a keyboard. From a relaxed campaign on Easy to a merciless one on Hard. Several players can share one device, each with their own campaign, records and hangar. Progress lives on your device and in your own private iCloud, never on our servers.

The Deal

The first 8 missions are free: full missions with real boss fights, not a tutorial on rails. If it is your game, one purchase opens the Full Version: all three campaigns, the endless mode and the Hard difficulty. NO ADS, no subscriptions, no energy timers, nothing to sign up for.

Plays OFFLINE: on a plane, in the metro, anywhere the signal drops. Game Center, iCloud sync, purchases and restores need a connection. The war does not.
