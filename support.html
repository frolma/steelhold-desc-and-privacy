Support & FAQ

Bought It On Another Device

Open the game, tap the gear in the corner of the title screen, scroll down to Support & Contact and tap Restore purchases. The Full Version belongs to your Apple ID, not to the device, and once it is on a device it keeps working without a connection.

Where Is My Progress

Progress travels through your own private iCloud. Sign both devices in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, open the game once on each while online, and give it a moment. The further run wins, so a device that fell behind never drags a campaign backwards; only a campaign you start over on purpose begins again. Every player travels with it. The look you picked in the hangar is chosen on each device, while what you earned follows you.

How The Free Part Works

The first 8 missions of Steel Crusade are free: full missions with real boss fights, and they pay out your first level rewards. One purchase opens the Full Version: Steel Crusade past the free missions, Iron Labyrinth and INFERNO from their first mission, Endless Onslaught, the Hard difficulty and the Slow, Fast and Turbo game speeds. NO ADS, no subscriptions.

Game Center And Leaderboards

Leaderboards and medals need Game Center switched on in your device settings and a connection at the moment they are sent. The game plays OFFLINE; a medal earned offline is offered to Game Center again after your next victory with a connection.

Several Players

In the MENU, tap Player to rename yourself, switch, or add another player. Each player has their own campaign, records and hangar. The Full Version and the sound settings are shared by everyone on the device.

Controllers And Keyboards

Pair a game controller or a keyboard in your device settings and the game picks it up. On a controller, drive with the D-pad or the left stick and fire with a face button or a trigger. On a keyboard, drive with WASD or the arrow keys and fire with Space.

I Want A Refund

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple usually answers within a day or two.

Sound Is Acting Up

Check the Music and Sound effects switches in the MENU, and the silent mode of your device. If something still sounds wrong, write to us. A short description plus your device model is enough.

About The Tips

The tips in the MENU are entirely optional, and each one says thank you in its own way. They never change how the game plays. Thank you. Seriously.
